YOUR FIRST STEP

Curious about CSI?

Start with an event. Meet the community. Find your way in.

Follow CSI VIT Pune ↗
Who can join?

Students at VIT Pune who are curious about technology, design, or building with others. Check CSI’s current announcements for membership and recruitment details.

How do I take part?

Follow the chapter for recruitment and event announcements, then use the registration link on the event post. You can also start by attending an open session or workshop.

What should I expect?

Hands-on workshops, hackathons, coding and debugging challenges, speaker sessions, and a team that learns by making things together. You don’t need to arrive as an expert.
